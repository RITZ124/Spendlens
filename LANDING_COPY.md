## FAQ

**Q: How does SpendLens know if I'm overpaying?**

A: We maintain a database of current pricing for every major AI tool — Cursor, ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot, Gemini, and more — sourced directly from vendor pricing pages and verified weekly. Our audit engine compares what you're paying against what makes sense for your team size and use case. The logic is rule-based, not AI-generated, so the math is always verifiable.

---

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**

A: No. You enter your tools, see your results, and optionally share your report — all without signing up. We only ask for your email if you want us to send you the report. Value first, contact info after.

---

**Q: Is my data private? What do you store?**

A: Your tool list and spend data are saved in your browser only (localStorage) while you're using the tool. If you choose to generate a shareable link, we store the audit results — tool names and savings numbers only, no personal information. If you submit your email for the report, that's stored separately and never included in the public share link.

---

**Q: How is this different from just looking at my invoices?**

A: Your invoices tell you what you paid. SpendLens tells you what you *should* be paying — and why. It knows that ChatGPT Team for 2 people is more expensive than two Plus plans. It knows that Cursor Business for a solo developer is overkill. It knows when your API usage patterns suggest a flat subscription would be cheaper. That's the layer of analysis that's missing when you're just looking at a bill.

---

**Q: What is Credex and why are they building this?**

A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, and others — sourced from companies that overforecast. The discount is real (20–40% below retail). SpendLens is free because it helps us find teams that are overspending and show them how Credex can help. If your audit shows significant savings, we'll reach out. If you're already spending well, we'll tell you that too.
